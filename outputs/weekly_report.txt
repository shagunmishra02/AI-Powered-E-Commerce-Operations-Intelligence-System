**Executive Summary**

The current state of our e-commerce operations indicates a mixed bag in terms of overall business health. On one hand, we have a substantial total customer base of 96,478, with 7,781 champions who are our most loyal and valuable customers. However, a deeper dive into the numbers reveals concerns, particularly with a significant portion of our customer base (41.3%) categorized as "At Risk" and a staggering 68,837 customers having already churned. This dynamic warrants careful attention to improve customer retention and overall customer lifecycle management.

Key risks and alerts have been identified through our anomaly detection systems and customer analytics. Notably, 149 anomalous sellers have been flagged, which may indicate potential issues with product quality, pricing, or seller behavior that could negatively impact customer satisfaction and trust in our platform. Furthermore, the worst delivery delay detected stands at 35.0 days, which is significantly above acceptable standards and could lead to increased customer dissatisfaction and churn. These risks, coupled with the high number of at-risk and churned customers, signal a need for immediate action to protect our customer base and maintain the quality of service expected from our platform.

To address these challenges, we recommend several actions for the operations team. Firstly, prioritize customer retention strategies targeting the 39,822 at-risk customers to prevent further churn. This could involve personalized marketing campaigns, enhanced customer support, and tailored promotions to re-engage these customers. Secondly, conduct a thorough investigation into the 149 flagged sellers to ensure compliance with our platform's standards and take corrective action as necessary. Lastly, implement measures to reduce delivery delays, such as optimizing logistics, improving inventory management, and potentially partnering with more reliable shipping providers. By addressing these key areas, we can work towards improving customer satisfaction, reducing churn, and ultimately enhancing the overall health and resilience of our e-commerce operations.
