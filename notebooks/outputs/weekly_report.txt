Additionally, the high number of at-risk customers warrants a proactive approach to prevent further churn and retain our customer base.

Thirdly, we should design targeted marketing campaigns and loyalty programs to re-engage our at-risk customers and prevent further churn. By taking these proactive steps, we can improve our overall business health, reduce risks, and drive long-term growth and profitability.
